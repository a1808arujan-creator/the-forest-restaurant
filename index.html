About The Forest

The Forest is a restaurant in Ust-Kamenogorsk created for relaxed dinners, family gatherings and special occasions.

Its atmosphere is inspired by nature and combines warm lighting, natural details and comfortable dining spaces.

Guests can explore a varied menu that includes cold starters, salads, chef's dishes, meat, fish, traditional bakery, desserts and drinks.
