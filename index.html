About The forest

The prominent The forest is a premium country restaurant located in Gornaya Ulbinka near Ust-Kamenagorsk,KZ.

Why visit The Forest

The restaurant offers a unique dining experience with a focus on high-quality ingredients, exceptional service, and a welcoming atmosphere. Guests can enjoy a variety of dishes, from traditional Kazakh cuisine to international favorites, all prepared by skilled chefs.
